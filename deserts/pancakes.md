# Pancakes!

Those are gluten and lactose free. An abomination? Maybe, but it means I can eat them so whatever. ¯\\\_(ツ)\_/¯

## Ingredients

- 2 cup of oat milk.
- 1.5 cups of gluten free flour.
- 1 tbs of vanilla — Yes, I like lots. YMMV.
- 1 large egg.

Mix in a bawl, cook pancakes in pan with (fake) butter, and enjoy eating them.

## Toppings

- Chocolate spread — a classic.
- Sugar, organe juice, and zest — and you can set fire to it! Flambe for the win.
- Whatever you'd like to put on them! I ain't the boss of you.
