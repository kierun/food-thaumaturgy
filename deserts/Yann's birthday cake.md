# Yann's Birthday Cake / Yanna tort urodzinowy

## Cake / Tort

- 6 eggs
- 200g sugar
- 75g grated chocolate
- 120g almond powder
- 50g bread crumbs

Mix egg yokes with sugar, fold stiffly beaten egg whites.

Add grated chocolate, almond powder, and bread crumbs.

Put into a well buttered mould and bake for about 40 mins in a medium hot oven.

It is best two days after preparation.

## Cream / Krem

- 2 egg yokes
- 130g sugar
- 170g butter
- 75g almond powder

Mix eggs yokes with sugar and heat heat (**do NOT boil**) till white and creamy.

Mix the butter with almond powder. Add it to the egg/sugar mix.

Decorate the cake.
