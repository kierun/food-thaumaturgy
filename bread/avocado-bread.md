# Avocado bread (1973)

[Dylan Hollis video, 1minute](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/LrPGbyXEUCw) if you want to
see how it's done.

- Two ripe avocados.
- 3/4 cup of sugar — That makes it very sweet, reducing is probably a good idea.
- 3 eggs.
- 2 cups of flour.
- 1.5 tsp of baking powder, double if making it gluten free.

Combine wet then add dry, fold, and cook at 180 for 45-55 mins in a bread tin.
