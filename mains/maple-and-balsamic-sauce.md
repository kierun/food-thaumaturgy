# Maple Syrup, Balsamic vinegar, and Soya Sauce Marinade

This is per person.

- 1 tbs maple syrup.
- 1 tbs balsamic vinegar.
- 1 tbs Soya sauce.
- 1 Chicken (or porc) fillets.

Cut meat in small pieces, and marinate in the marinade for as long as you can — 10 minutes is better than nothing, hours
are great.

Take the meat out of the marinade, and cook it until it is done. Add the marinade and reduce.

Serve with whatever you like: potatoes, pasta, rice, vegetables…
